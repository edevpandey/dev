Sinko Privacy Policy

Effective: 27 November 2026 App: Sinko (com.edev.sinko) Developer: Dev Pandey (edev), Kathmandu, Nepal

The short version

Sinko has no account and no analytics, and we (the developer) do not collect, store or receive any of your data. Your input, files, scans, documents and vault never leave your phone except to the PC or phone you send them to, over Bluetooth or your own local Wi-Fi. The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob and downloads short announcements from Google Firebase. Those Google services receive some device information, as described below.

This policy explains what Sinko ("the app") does with your information. "We", "us" and "our" mean Dev Pandey (edev), the developer and publisher of the app.

1. Information we collect

We collect nothing. We do not run any server that the app talks to, and we never receive your input, files, scans, documents, vault contents, contacts or location.

Two Google services built into the app, Google AdMob (ads) and Google Firebase Remote Config (announcements), receive some device information to work. They are described under Third parties.

2. How the app uses data on your device

Everything below happens on your phone, or directly between your phone and a device you chose to connect.

  • Input you send. Key presses, mouse movement, typed text, media keys and scanned codes go directly from your phone to the PC you connected, over Bluetooth or over your local Wi-Fi network.
  • Files and text you share. These go directly between your phone and the PC or phone you chose, over your local Wi-Fi network. Received files are saved in Download/Sinko or the folder you chose. The app reads only the files you pick or share into it.
  • Fast Transfer. To move files faster, Sinko can link two devices directly over a local Wi-Fi connection (Wi-Fi Direct or a local hotspot). The network name and password are shown as a QR code, or sent over Bluetooth, only to the device you are connecting. The connection is local and does not use the internet.
  • Camera. Used only while the Scanner screen or the document camera is open. For barcodes and QR codes, images are processed on the phone and are never saved or sent; only the text of the code is used. Pages you scan as documents are saved in the app's storage on your phone. They go to your PC only when you send them, or as you scan while "To PC" / Live to PC is switched on, over your local Wi-Fi network.
  • Documents and PDFs. Scanned documents, PDFs you open or create, and your signatures are stored on your phone. Page detection and all image processing run on the phone, using an on-device model; no images are uploaded.
  • Vault. Documents, PDFs, photos and videos you move into the vault are encrypted on your phone with a key derived from your vault password. The password and the recovery code are not stored and never sent anywhere, so nobody, including us, can open the vault without them. The app reads only the photos, videos and files you pick or share to "Sinko Vault". After a shared file is safely in the vault, the app asks Android to delete the original. For items in your gallery, Android shows its own confirmation first. Originals the app is not allowed to delete stay where they were, and the app tells you so.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock. Optional, for the vault. The check is done by Android; the app never sees your fingerprint or face data. When it is on, a copy of the vault key is kept in Android's secure key storage on this phone, where only that check can release it.
  • Motion sensors. The gyroscope is read only while the Mouse screen is open, to move the pointer. Sensor data is not stored or sent anywhere except as pointer movement to your PC.
  • Local network. Used to find and talk to your PC (running Sinko Server) and other phones running Sinko on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Notifications. A notification is shown while the phone is connected as a keyboard and mouse, so the connection can stay active in the background.

3. Permissions and why they are needed

Android asks you before granting the sensitive permissions below, and you can revoke them at any time in Settings → Apps → Sinko → Permissions. Features that need a permission you deny simply won't work; the rest of the app keeps working.

Sinko does not request access to your contacts, call log, SMS, microphone or precise location data.

4. Information stored on your device

Your settings, the pairing key for your PC, the list of phones you chose to always accept, your scanned documents and the vault are stored on your phone only. Uninstalling the app deletes them, including everything in the vault: move items out of the vault first if you want to keep them. Files you received or exported stay in your Downloads, Pictures or Movies folders until you delete them.

5. Third parties

The app contains no analytics software. It uses the following Google services, each under Google's Privacy Policy:

Google AdMob (ads)

Some screens show banner ads. To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), basic device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. Ads never see your input, files, scans, documents or vault. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads), and turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Google Firebase Remote Config (announcements)

The app downloads short news and update messages shown on its home page. To do this, Firebase uses a random app-installation ID and basic device and app information (such as the app version). It is not linked to your name or to anything you send to your PC. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Google Play

The app is distributed and updated through Google Play. It asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, and that check is handled by Google.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share any data with anyone else.

6. Security

Data you send to your PC or phone travels only over Bluetooth or your local network. Connections to a PC use a pairing key stored on your phone. The vault is encrypted on the device, and its key never leaves the phone. Data exchanged with Google services is encrypted in transit by Google. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but Sinko is designed so that your content never reaches the internet.

7. Your choices and data deletion

  • Delete everything: uninstall Sinko. All app data on your phone (settings, pairing keys, scans and the vault) is deleted. We hold no copy, so there is nothing else to delete on our side.
  • Clear data without uninstalling: Settings → Apps → Sinko → Storage → Clear data.
  • Advertising: reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, as described above.
  • Permissions: revoke any permission at any time in Android settings.

8. Children

Sinko is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13. We collect no personal information from anyone. The ads and announcements described above are provided by Google.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
