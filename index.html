Developer, teacher & builder

I'm Dev Pandey, also known as DevSir, a software developer based in Kathmandu, Nepal. I love turning ideas into products that people actually use every day.

Beyond code, I teach accounting and write technical content. Teaching keeps my explanations simple, and my apps simpler.
